1. Reference images

Front, side and back views of one person, head to soles, on a plain contrasting background. Front only works too, in a basic mode.

How to prepare reference images
  • All views: same character, same scale and framing, full body from the top of the hair to the shoe soles, centred. PNG, at least 1024 px tall.
  • Background: one flat colour that contrasts with hair, skin and every piece of clothing (no grey backdrop for grey clothes). No gradients, vignettes or floor shadows.
  • Camera: orthographic, or a long lens from far away. Even, neutral lighting.
  • Front: facing the camera, relaxed A-pose with the arms a little away from the torso, legs straight and slightly apart.
  • Side: an exact 90° profile, standing (not mid-stride), arms visible, feet flat.
  • Back: a direct rear view showing the hair length and the back of the clothes; no face visible.
  • Hard backgrounds: add a mask per view (white figure on black) with + mask.
